# Frontier Capital Partners — website brand application

Applied 9 November 2026 from the saved 22 October Brand System v1. The approved artwork is used directly, not retyped or redrawn.

## Sources and implementation

Source: Frontier-Brand-System-v1.zip and Frontier-Capital-Partners-Brand-Book-v1.pdf. The Library masters are unchanged. The site includes the supplied navy horizontal vector at assets/logo.svg, the supplied white horizontal vector and both bull variants in assets/brand/. Original paths, proportions and lettering are retained.

- Navy #0D233D, tint #193853, white #FFFFFF and paper #F4F6F8.
- Canonical broad radial gradient centred at 50% / 12%, easing to navy at 80%.
- Navy logo on white headers; white logo on navy footer. Narrow phones use the supplied bull with the accessible company name, rather than squeezing the full wordmark.
- Arial / Helvetica / sans-serif for live type. The outlined serif lettering remains exclusive to the approved logo. No new premium font selection is implied.
- White homepage headline on navy; approved “Cross the frontier. Funding your ambition.” pairing and working descriptor. Other editorial content remains editable placeholders.
- Navigation, search, mobile menu, business selector, directory/archive filters, notices, map and footers share the Frontier palette.
- Placeholder image slots remain; only their neutral colour treatment has changed.

assets/brand.css owns the brand layer after replica.css. The build maps captured reference colour tokens to Frontier tokens in generated CSS/HTML; source reference files remain intact. This maintains the existing page hierarchy and interactions while separating the visual identity.

## Verification

The 114-route build passes checks of 9,999 local links and 3,023 asset references, JavaScript syntax and disconnected forms. Browser checks cover 24 desktop templates and 48 phone/tablet template views. No broken loaded images or horizontal page overflow were found after correcting Ventures/Crypto split-banner geometry. Mobile menu has the supplied logo, canonical gradient, all 18 links and working local search. Existing form integrations remain disconnected.

This is a first website brand application for review, not production approval. Photography/video, final copy, service taxonomy and any premium typeface choice remain later decisions. External businesses and portal destinations remain labelled placeholders. The supplied bull favicon is provisional; the brand book's small-size optical refinement remains open.
