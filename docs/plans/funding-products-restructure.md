# Funding products and sector navigation implementation plan

Date: 17 November 2026
Branch: funding-products-restructure
Status: implementation plan; no customer-facing changes made
Baseline: main at c165b6270fb59a3a294bff1faebca704faafe793

## Objective

Help visitors find a recognisable finance product after one mobile menu expansion. Separate products, customer goals and industry sectors. Preserve Frontier's wider advisory, equity and specialist proposition alongside the new equipment offering.

## 1. Navigation and information architecture

Funding Solutions opens a single list in this agreed order:

1. Business Loans
2. Asset & Equipment Finance
3. Invoice Finance
4. Property Finance
5. Bridging Loans
6. Stock & Dealer Finance
7. Trade & Import Finance
8. Acquisition & Buyout Finance
9. Asset Based Lending
10. Equity & Growth Capital

Follow with All Funding Solutions and Help choosing a route. Use this order consistently in desktop/mobile navigation and the main product directory. This is an editorial order based on likely target-customer demand, not a verified market usage or popularity ranking.

On the Asset & Equipment Finance hub, use this order: Hire Purchase; Equipment Leasing; Asset Refinance; Vehicle & Fleet Finance; Finance for Your Customers. These are linked pages, not a second mobile-menu accordion. On the Plant & Machinery sector page, prioritise equipment acquisition, asset refinance, invoice finance and stock finance to suit that audience.

Business Loans links to a Business Loans & Working Capital page: distinguish term loans, revolving credit and overdrafts visibly, with separate mechanics, repayment and renewal considerations. Product availability through Frontier remains subject to provider agreements.

Remove the nested Funding and sectors accordion and the duplicate goal links from the menu. Keep customer goals on /solutions and the homepage, linking them to relevant products. Do not add a second accordion inside the mobile Funding Solutions list. Product details live on landing pages and their linked child pages.

Add a separate Sectors destination in desktop and mobile navigation with a sector index. Initial explicit sectors: Plant & Machinery, Technology, Life Sciences and Insurance. Retain a discoverable Digital Asset Businesses specialist page with accurately scoped copy. Sector pages link to applicable products; they must not imply that every product is available to every sector.

Retain Sustainability & Impact as a cross-cutting specialist theme, linked from relevant equipment and equity pages and the solutions directory. Retain Complex Funding as a specialist route in the solutions directory and Funding Guide; do not label it a sector or discard its existing page.

## 3. Copy approach and evidence

Use Lombard's public pages as research references for product mechanics, asset coverage and questions to address. Write original Frontier copy, with Frontier's own structure, examples and voice. Do not copy or closely paraphrase Lombard prose or present its offering as Frontier's established facility.

Each product page: clear customer benefit; what it is; who it may suit; typical uses; how the structure works; ownership/security/end-of-term commitments; information needed; related alternatives; concise FAQs; enquiry form. Keep paragraphs short and explanations practical. Existing brand, typography and page components remain the design starting point.

Frontier arranges introductions rather than lending its own money. Distinguish provider-paid introductions from separately commissioned client-paid advisory services. Do not invent an agreed Lombard relationship, panel acceptance, exclusivity, rates, minimum/maximum amounts, decision times, acceptance criteria, tax outcomes or guaranteed savings. Publish specific commercial claims only after verification and confirmation they apply to Frontier's channel.

Chattel mortgages remain a specialist explanation where relevant, not a general machinery promise.

### Page-by-page writing and rewriting scope

Writing is a substantive deliverable for every new page and every retained page affected by the restructure, not a relabelling exercise or a generic template with product names swapped. Prepare complete publication-ready copy in the existing content format before final integration. Preserve the approved visual language and use existing page components where they serve the explanation.

| --- | --- |

| Asset & Equipment Finance | Original hub copy explaining acquisition, use and releasing asset value; link child pages in agreed order |

| Homepage, Funding Guide, footer and navigation | Rewrite affected labels, summaries and recommendations to match the taxonomy |

For each substantive product page, supply a short category label, benefit-led headline, concise introduction, primary CTA, suitable-use examples, practical explanation, key commitments, tailored FAQs, next-step copy, related-page links, SEO title and meta description. Reuse the existing format's suitability, purpose, how-it-works, commitments and questions sections where appropriate. Remove repetitive or unnecessary sections rather than padding every page to the same length.

Frontier voice: plain British English, calm, direct and commercially useful. Lead with what the customer can achieve and use concrete business examples. Explain technical terms once. Avoid jargon-heavy headlines, repeated generic ambition language, inflated promises and lengthy precautionary copy that obscures the product. Keep necessary qualifications close to the claim they explain. Maintain existing fee and information-sharing disclosures accurately and proportionately.

Research provider pages appropriate to each product (Lombard for asset products; relevant primary-provider sources for invoice finance, trade finance, loans and ABL). Record source URLs, access date, supported factual points and unresolved questions in an internal source register. Provider research informs product facts and explanatory coverage; the final wording, examples, structure and Frontier proposition must be original. Verify current facts when drafting; do not infer availability of a product from another group's website or a generic broker-panel page.

Related services: bad-debt protection/trade-credit insurance, foreign exchange and equipment insurance remain distinct from funding products. Explain only where relevant and where the service route is confirmed; do not create unsupported service promises. The scope now explicitly covers all product requirements in the shared master briefing, with contractual variants explained within their family rather than each receiving a top-level menu item.
