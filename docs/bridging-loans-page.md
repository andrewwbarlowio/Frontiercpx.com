# Bridging Loans page

## Scope
Business-purpose temporary funding, including gaps before a funding round, cash receipts, transactions or refinancing. Use the exact product label **Bridging Loans**. Not limited to property. Individual eligibility, structure and provider appetite are established in conversation.

## Research and editorial decisions — 13 November 2026

- British Business Bank, business bridging explainer: https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/business-guidance/guidance-articles/finance/what-is-a-business-bridging-loan . Supports a broad temporary business funding purpose and the need to address repayment, costs and security. We do not adopt its generalised early repayment claims or its crowdfunding description.
- Funding Circle, short-term business loans: https://www.fundingcircle.com/uk/small-business-loans/short-term-business-loans/bridging-loans/ . Demonstrates business uses beyond property and provider-specific pricing, assessment and guarantees. No lender rates, limits, turnaround promises or relationships are imported into Frontier copy.
- British Business Bank, historic Future Fund evaluation announcement: https://www.british-business-bank.co.uk/news-and-events/news/half-future-fund-recipients-say-their-businesses-may-have-closed-without-its-support-finds-new . Establishes historical use of bridge financing before an equity round. The closed scheme is not marketed as an available product. A conventional loan and a convertible structure must not be presented as interchangeable.

Headline direction: keep the business moving while the next capital is arranged. Supporting copy leads with purpose, duration and evidence behind the exit. An expected round is not a guaranteed repayment source. No universal eligibility, unsecured availability, rate, loan size or completion time is claimed.
