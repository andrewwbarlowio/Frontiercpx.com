# Frontier credibility improvement plan
10 November 2026 · Preview implementation · Four connected pillars

## Objective
Make Frontier’s reach, expertise, access to capital and delivery approach visible without publishing Andy’s or Roger’s biographies. Retain the approved navy brand, concise commercial language and goal-led funding journeys.

| Pillar | Implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Global Presence | Dedicated page with San Francisco, New York, London, Abu Dhabi and Singapore; shortlisted provider addresses; coordinated city cards and abstract skyline artwork. No per-city service promises. |
| Our Expertise | Dedicated capability page covering assessment, preparation, structuring and execution. Prominent scale indicators. Roger’s founder-supplied 35 years in financial services supports experience wording, not an assertion of 35 years in leadership. |
| Frontier Funding Network | Branded capital-access page covering banks, specialist lenders, private credit, equity, family offices and property/project capital; explain purpose, provider fit and matching process. No invented capital pool or claim of money under management. |
| Our Approach | Bring the existing principles and process into one destination: understand, assess, shape, approach and support the decision. Preserve clear responsibilities, commercial arrangements and information handling. |

## Delivery
1. Build the four pages in the existing customer-journey preview.
2. Add homepage cards, About-page connections, desktop/mobile navigation and footer links.
3. Keep existing How it works URLs functional; direct primary approach navigation to the unified page.
4. Validate the complete generated site and commit the changes on the existing preview branch. Do not merge to main.

## Evidence and launch dependencies
- Five addresses are proposed. Confirm workspace contracts, permitted address use, exact mailing formats and operational status before removing draft labels. New York is not labelled headquarters until that role is established.
- The 100+ specialists and 250+ lending relationships are user-requested illustrative design targets, visibly labelled in preview. They are not Fuse figures or current Frontier claims. Replace with evidenced, accessible network counts or remove.
- Confirm Roger’s financial-services experience record. No individual biographies are required.
- Andy and Roger source and agree delivery/funding partnerships: scope, client ownership, fees, specialist availability, information sharing and permitted credentials.
- Partner credentials and prior transactions must retain accurate attribution. A partner’s past fundraising is not Frontier’s own track record.
- Keep preview noindex and disconnected enquiry forms. Live enquiry delivery, service coverage, privacy, production QA and release remain separate dependencies.

## Acceptance
All four routes render without JavaScript; internal links and assets resolve; existing funding journeys and forms retain their preview behaviour; draft facts remain clearly labelled; mobile layouts use single-column cards where needed; no personal biographies or fictional staff are added.
